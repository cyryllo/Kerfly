# Privacy policy

Kerfly is a program that runs on your computer. It has no account, no
advertising and no telemetry: it does not collect, store or send data about
you or your work.

## What Kerfly sends over the network

- **New versions.** Once a day, when Kerfly starts, it asks GitHub for the
  latest Kerfly release (`api.github.com/repos/cyryllo/Kerfly/releases/latest`).
  The request carries the Kerfly version (in its User-Agent, for example
  `Kerfly/1.8.0 (update check)`) and nothing about you, your computer or your
  projects. As with any web request, GitHub sees your IP address; GitHub's own
  [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
  applies. Switch it off in **Settings → Advanced → Check once a day for a new
  version**. **Help → Check for updates** asks only when you click it.
- **Nothing else** goes out by itself. **Help → Report a problem on GitHub…**
  saves a diagnostic report on your computer and opens GitHub's issue form in
  your browser (with the Kerfly version, system and board in the address);
  nothing is sent unless you fill in and submit that form yourself and attach
  the report.

## What Kerfly keeps on your computer

Your settings, material library, laser profiles, the diagnostic log and the
pictures it makes (stamps, cut-out pictures) are kept in your user's settings
folder. Projects are saved where you choose. Nothing of this leaves your
computer unless you send it.
