The third release candidate (RC3) of version 2.0. Changes since RC2:

### Changed

- **A double-click on a shape with a living hinge or a fill** opens its
  **Living hinge** or **Fill** window again (it opened its nodes; they stay
  under **Edit nodes** on the ribbon).

### Fixed

- **Ctrl+Z while drawing with the pen or a polyline** took back the step
  before the drawing (a shape drawn earlier went), and after it nothing could
  be drawn any more. Now Ctrl+Z takes back the last node, as Backspace.
- **A shape with a living hinge or a fill resized** (handles, W / H in the
  bar) had its cuts or pattern stretched with it (the hinge's rows far
  apart). Now the shape takes the new size and its hinge or pattern is made
  again, keeping its own lengths and gaps.
- **Nest on the bed: the progress window** cut its text at the left with some
  system fonts.

Full list of the 2.0 changes: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/cyryllo/Kerfly/blob/2.0rc3/CHANGELOG.md).
