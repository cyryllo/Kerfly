The fifth beta of version 2.0. Changes since Beta 4:

### Changed

- **The tools' windows** (as in Vintyl): **Fill**, **Living hinge**,
  **Offset**, **Text** and **Text on a path** are set up in a window:
  - on the left a **preview of your shape** that follows every change;
  - on the right the kinds as **tiles with pictures** (19 fill patterns,
    3 kinds of hinge cuts, round / sharp corners) and the fields with icons;
  - at the bottom **Cancel** and the action (**Fill it**, **Cut the hinge**,
    **Offset**, **Insert the text**), on the left **Remove** where there is
    something to remove.

  Nothing changes on the bed before the action is clicked (one undo step). A
  tool opens its window on a click on a shape, or at once for the shapes
  selected; **Text** on a click on the bed; a **double-click on a text** opens
  it again.
- **The options bar** keeps the quick things (position, size, rotation,
  layer, corners, bridges) and has **Fill…**, **Living hinge…** and **Edit
  text…** buttons for the windows of the shape shown.

### Removed

- **Apply / Cancel in the options bar**: the tools' windows have them now.
  Changes in the bar show at once and Ctrl+Z takes each one back.

Full list of the 2.0 changes: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/cyryllo/Kerfly/blob/2.0b5/CHANGELOG.md).
