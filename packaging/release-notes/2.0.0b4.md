The fourth beta of version 2.0. Changes since Beta 3 (after the laser tests of 20 November):

### Changed

- **Apply / Cancel in the options bar**: changes show at once, **Cancel**
  takes back every change since the bar showed this shape or tool (also the
  hinges and fills a tool gave), **Apply** keeps them and ends the tool. The
  buttons are always in sight on the right.
- **No sideways scroll bar in the options bar**: in a smaller window the fields
  go on in more lines and the bar grows downwards.
- **Nest on the bed: Parts Selected / All** when only some parts are selected
  (one part left selected by chance made the rest stay where they were).
- **GRBL homing speed** removed from the settings: the board keeps its own
  (`$25`). Betas 2 and 3 wrote it on connect when it was set: check `$25` in
  LaserGRBL if your board now homes at another speed.

### Fixed

- **Living hinge** (the tool, the test card, the rounded box): the plywood did
  not bend, as a solid border ran along the edges. Now short cuts reach the
  edges, the long ones keep a bridge at both ends, neighbouring rows' bridges
  never line up, and the margin is left only at the hinge's ends.
- **Cut order**: a shape inside another element's cut (a circle drawn after
  the square it sits in, grouped or not) is cut first.
- **"Off the bed" for turned shapes**: their lines count, not the rectangle
  round the turned shape.
- **Shared material library**: a laser that starts sharing another one's
  library brings its own materials into it (they seemed to vanish).
- **Crashes on Linux** (at exit, sometimes while pasting or nesting): fields
  of the bar and panel removed the safe way, the program ends at once. Should
  it still crash, the next start writes where in the log (Help → Report a
  problem).

Full list of the 2.0 changes: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/cyryllo/Kerfly/blob/2.0b4/CHANGELOG.md).
