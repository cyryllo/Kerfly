The second release candidate (RC2) of version 2.0. Changes since RC1 (after the laser tests of 21 November):

### Added

- **Short moves between cuts without stopping (K40, beta)**: Settings →
  Lasers… → **Moves between cuts**. A K40 makes every move between two cuts
  as a fast move: it stops, leaves the cutting mode and jumps off (at each
  bridge of a living hinge the head seemed to go off). Moves up to the length
  set (for example 5 mm) are made at the cutting speed with the laser off,
  without stopping. **0** (the default): the laser data as before.

### Fixed

- **Text on a path window**: its preview drew the path a text's height away
  from the text (on the bed it was right).
- **A turned shape drawn red**: it was marked "outside the work area" when
  the rectangle round it stuck out of the bed though its lines were on it.
  Now its lines count, as in the job's check.

Full list of the 2.0 changes: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/cyryllo/Kerfly/blob/2.0rc2/CHANGELOG.md).
